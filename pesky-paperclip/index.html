Pesky Paperclip

Twisty Toy-Box Puzzle · Coming soon to iPhone

It’s tidy-up time on the desk. Each glossy piece has to leave through the exit of its colour, but the exits stay put while the board turns: one twist lines up blue and throws orange out of reach. Paperclips sit across the exits and ride round with the board, so you twist them away, or drag one onto the board, where it stays for good. Short, timed boards that make you think two moves ahead.

One straight run of levels, no map. New ideas arrive as you go: paperclips, pieces that hide their real colour underneath, and exits that change colour on the clock. Between lessons the game picks each board to fit how you’re playing, with an easier one after a struggle and a harder one when you’re cruising. Every win puts the toys away in the toy chest and earns buttons for desk toys and themes.
