Support

Herdwise, Deepwell and Zenolith

Something not working, a puzzle that seems wrong, or an idea? Write to us:

support@ambiguous.com

It helps if you tell us which game, which iPhone and which version of iOS you have, and what happened. A screenshot is often the quickest way to show us.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone, inside the game. Deleting a game deletes its progress, so keep the game installed if you want to keep your place.

A Herdwise board seems impossible

Every board has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by reasoning, without guessing. If you’re stuck, check that no paddock has every cell crossed out (a cross is only a note, and you can clear it), or use a hint. If you still think a board is wrong, send us a screenshot and we’ll look.
