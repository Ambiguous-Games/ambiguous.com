Deepwell

Neon Arcade · Coming soon to iPhone

Deepwell is a neon arcade game for one thumb. Coloured creatures creep up the lanes of a glowing vector well toward the rim where you stand. Tap a lane to fire your colour into it: three or more of a colour touching pop, the lanes fall back, and pops can chain.

Chain three to earn a laser that blasts a whole lane. Swipe to spin the well and flick to keep it turning. Play Endless for a high score, the Daily Well that everyone gets the same, or the Journey, all to a quiet, hypnotic techno pulse.
