Round Trip

Space Port Puzzle · Coming soon to iPhone

Aliens are spiralling down the station tower, and every one of them wants a ride. Tap a ship and it slides off the lot to a launch pad, fills up with aliens of its own colour and flies them home. Then it comes back. Every returning ship parks on the lot again, often right where it gets in your way, so clearing the lot is also filling it. Send the right ship at the right moment, keep the pads moving, and don’t box yourself in.

75 levels across five planet ports, each with its own station and its own lot shape, drawn in a bright 1960s space-age cartoon style. Every so often an alien asks you a favour. Say yes and you’ll get a thank-you card for your album, and now and then a spare launch pad for a tight spot.
