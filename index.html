Ambiguous Games is a small studio in Ontario, Canada, making puzzle and arcade games for iPhone.

Herdwise

Animal Logic Puzzle

Settle every animal in its paddock: one in each row, each column and each paddock, and no two touching, not even at the corners. Numbered rocks, a kangaroo, an owl and a mole bend the rules along the way.

Coming soon to iPhone

Deepwell

Neon Arcade

Coloured creatures creep up a neon well toward you. Fire your colour into a lane: three or more touching pop, and pops chain. Don’t let a lane reach the rim.

Coming soon to iPhone

Zenolith

Koans of the Necker

Every piece is a Necker cube, and every Necker cube can be seen two ways. Choose how each one is read, set it in the tower, and let the light climb.

Coming soon to iPhone
