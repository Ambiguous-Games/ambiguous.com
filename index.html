Thoughtful games for iPhone.

A small studio in Toronto, Canada, making puzzle and arcade games for iPhone.

Herdwise

Animal Logic Puzzle

Settle every animal in its paddock: one in each row, each column and each paddock, and no two touching, not even at the corners. Numbered rocks, a kangaroo, an owl and a mole bend the rules along the way.

Coming soon to iPhone

Zenolith

Koans of the Necker · Calm Light Puzzle

Lead coloured light up a dark round tower, one ring at a time. Flip a cube to its other reading to turn its pipe, and lay a bridge where two colours must cross. The tower you light is yours.

Coming soon to iPhone

Pesky Paperclip

Twisty Toy-Box Puzzle

Slide each toy out through the exit of its colour, before the clock runs out. Turn the whole board a quarter at a time to line things up, and work round the pesky paperclips sitting in the way.

Coming soon to iPhone

Round Trip

Space Port Puzzle

Tap ships off a crowded launch lot to fly the aliens home. The catch: every ship comes back and parks again, so each one you send out is a blocker you’ll have to plan around.

Coming soon to iPhone

Deepwell

Neon Arcade

Coloured creatures creep up a neon well toward you. Fire your colour into a lane: three or more touching pop, and pops chain. Don’t let a lane reach the rim.

Coming soon to iPhone
