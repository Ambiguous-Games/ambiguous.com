Zenolith

Koans of the Necker · Calm Light Puzzle · Coming soon to iPhone

Zenolith is a calm puzzle about light. Each colour starts at a source in a round stone tower and has to reach its target, one ring at a time. You place cubes that carry pipes, and you can flip any cube you’ve placed to its other reading, a true Necker reversal that sends its pipe another way. Where two colours must cross, a bridge carries one over the other. There are no timers and no fail state, and you can always undo.

The climb opens with koans: small rings you solve in the dark, then light with one tap. Two chapters of koans bring in red, blue and amber. From chapter 3 the open climb deals you a hand of pieces, and green joins in chapter 4. Every ring you light stays where it is, and the chapters stack into one tower: your own zenolith, glowing against the night.
