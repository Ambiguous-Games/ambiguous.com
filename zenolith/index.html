Zenolith

Koans of the Necker · Coming soon to iPhone

Zenolith is a quiet puzzle about seeing things two ways. Each piece is a Necker cube, the drawing that flips between two readings as you look at it, and each reading routes a pipe differently. Set the pieces in a dark round tower and find the readings that carry the light from the core up through every ring.

The climb opens with koans, small puzzles that each hold one idea, then becomes a free climb. Every ring you light becomes part of your own monolith, which grows chapter by chapter against the night.
