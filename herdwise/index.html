Herdwise

Animal Logic Puzzle · Coming soon to iPhone

Herdwise is a logic puzzle with a herd. Every board is a field of coloured paddocks, and every animal needs a place: one in each row, one in each column, one in each paddock, and no two animals touching, not even at the corners. Every board has exactly one answer, and you can always reason your way to it.

The journey crosses the Savanna, the Outback, the Wetlands and the Forest, with new animals and new twists in each: rocks that count their neighbours, a kangaroo that may touch, an owl that watches its diagonals, a mole that hides under a rock. Solve boards to earn hay and build your herd a zoo. A new Daily board arrives every day.
