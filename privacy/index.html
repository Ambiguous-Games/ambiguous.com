Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy covers the games made by Ambiguous Games Inc. (“we”): Herdwise, Deepwell and Zenolith, and this website, ambiguous.com.

The short version

Our games don’t collect personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you do in a game is sent to us.

What the games store

Your progress, settings and scores are saved on your device, inside the game. They stay there and are deleted when you delete the game. If your device backs up to iCloud or your computer, Apple includes this data in those backups under Apple’s privacy policy; we can’t see it.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind, and we don’t sell or share data.
  • The games don’t need an internet connection, and they don’t contact our servers.

When you write to us

If you email support@ambiguous.com, we receive your email address and whatever you send. We use it only to answer you, and we don’t add you to any mailing list.

This website

ambiguous.com sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

Our games are suitable for all ages. Because the games collect no personal information, they collect none from children.

Changes

If a future version of a game adds something that uses data, such as advertising, in-app purchases, leaderboards or iCloud sync, we’ll update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
